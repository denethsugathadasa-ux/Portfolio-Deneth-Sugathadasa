Cyber Safety — For High School Students

STAY SAFE
ONLINE.

The internet is part of your life — school, gaming, social media, everything. But one bad click can change everything. Here's what you need to know.

01 🔐

Use strong, unique passwords

Never reuse passwords across accounts. Use a passphrase like PurpleDog$Runs7 and a free password manager like Bitwarden.

02 📲

Turn on two-factor authentication

Enable 2FA on Instagram, Snapchat, Gmail and TikTok. Even if someone gets your password, they still can't get in.

03 🔒

Lock down your privacy settings

Set your social accounts to private. Strangers don't need to see your location, school, or daily routine.

04 🤔

Think before you post

Anything you post online can stay there forever — even after you delete it. Future employers and universities can see it.

🚨

Never share personal info with strangers online

Your full name, school, address, phone number and photos should never be shared with people you haven't met in real life — no matter how friendly they seem. Online predators build trust slowly before they ask.

🚩 Red flags — spot these instantly

Someone you just met online asks to move the chat to a private app — this is a grooming tactic

A message says you won a prize or gift card — click nothing, it's a scam

A link from a "friend" looks weird or unexpected — their account may be hacked, verify before clicking

Anyone online asks for photos, your location, or to meet in person — tell a trusted adult immediately

Something feel wrong online?
Tell a parent, teacher or trusted adult. You won't get in trouble.
